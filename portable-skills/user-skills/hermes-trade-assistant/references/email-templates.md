## 一、开发信核心原则

### 决定邮件命运的4个关卡

```
标题（决定是否打开）
  → 预览窗格前3行（决定是否继续读）
    → 正文内容（决定是否感兴趣）
      → Call to Action（决定是否回复）
```

### 12条垃圾邮件过滤规则（触发了就会进垃圾箱）

1. ❌ 全部大写（FREE SAMPLE, BEST PRICE）
2. ❌ 连续感叹号（!!!）或问号（???）
3. ❌ 红色字体或异常大字体
4. ❌ 只有图片没有文字（或图片占比过高）
5. ❌ 使用垃圾触发词：Free, Discount, Limited Offer, Act Now, Click Here, Buy Now
6. ❌ 主题行用 "Re:" 假装回复
7. ❌ 主题行全部大写
8. ❌ 过多链接（超过3个）
9. ❌ 附件过大（首次开发信不要带附件）
10. ❌ 用词过于推销语气
11. ❌ HTML 代码过于复杂
12. ❌ 发送频率过高（同一主题短时间内大量发送）

### 5条提升回复率的技巧

1. **发送多封邮件（跟进）**: 数据表明发2-3封跟进邮件可将回复率提升3倍
2. **个性化**: 提到客户公司名、网站、近期新闻
3. **简短**: 3-5句话足够，没人读长篇大论
4. **明确CTA**: 你想让客户做什么？（回复？约电话？要报价？）
5. **发送时间**: 对方当地时间周二-周四 8:00-10:00 或 15:00-16:00

## 二、金牌开发信模板

### 模板1 — "稳定供应商"策略

```
Subject: LED Lighting Products - Alternative Supplier for You

Dear [Name],

I'm [Your Name] from [Company], specializing in [Product] for over [X] years.
[Well-known client A] and [Well-known client B] are our distributors.

I understand you have stable suppliers, but having an alternative source
can strengthen your negotiating position with your current suppliers.

I checked your website and noticed we have many similar products.
Would you like me to send our catalogue and price list for comparison?

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Company]
[Photo]
```

**为什么有效**: 不攻击现有供应商，而是提供"备选方案"，客户无压力。

---

### 模板2 — "打过电话"策略

```
Subject: Following up on my call - [Product]

Dear [Name],

This is [Name] from [Company]. I tried calling your office but wasn't
able to reach you.

We specialize in [Product] and I noticed some overlap with your current
product line. I'd love to share our latest catalogue and pricing with you.

Would you be open to a brief comparison?

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**为什么有效**: "打过电话"显得更有诚意，不是群发。

---

### 模板3 — "我发现你们网站"策略

```
Subject: [Specific observation about their business]

Dear [Name],

I came across [Company] while researching [Industry/Market] and was
impressed by your [specific product line / recent achievement].

We manufacture [Product] and have helped companies similar to yours
[achieve specific benefit, e.g. reduce sourcing costs by 20%].

Would you be interested in seeing how our [Product] compares to
what you're currently using?

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**为什么有效**: 证明你真的研究过他们，不是群发。

---

### 模板4 — 行业洞察策略

```
Subject: [Industry trend/statistic] - quick thought

Dear [Name],

I recently read that [relevant industry statistic or trend].
Given your focus on [their market/product], I thought this
might be relevant.

Our [Product] addresses exactly this by [key benefit].
Happy to share more details if this resonates.

Best,
[Name]
```

**为什么有效**: 用行业数据开场，显示专业性。

---

### 模板5 — 简短直接策略

```
Subject: [Product] manufacturer - quick intro

Hi [Name],

We're a [Product] manufacturer based in [City], China.
- [Key Fact 1: e.g. 10 years experience]
- [Key Fact 2: e.g. CE/RoHS certified]
- [Key Fact 3: e.g. Serving 50+ distributors worldwide]

Would a quick comparison of our pricing vs your current supplier
be valuable?

Cheers,
[Name]
```

**为什么有效**: 极简，3秒读完，要点清晰。

## 三、跟进邮件模板

### 第1封跟进（3天后）

```
Subject: Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

Just following up on my previous email. I know you're busy,
but wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.

I've attached a one-page summary of our bestselling [Product]
with FOB pricing for your reference.

Any feedback would be appreciated!

Best,
[Name]
```

### 第2封跟进（7天后）

```
Subject: Quick question about [Product/Industry]

Hi [Name],

I was wondering — what's the biggest challenge you face when
sourcing [Product] from China?

We've been helping clients with [specific problem] and I thought
our experience might be relevant to you.

No pressure — just curious if this resonates.

Best,
[Name]
```

### 第3封跟进（14天后）— 破冰

```
Subject: Should I close your file?

Hi [Name],

I've tried reaching you a couple of times about [Product].
I don't want to keep bothering you if this isn't a priority
right now.

If the timing isn't right, just let me know and I'll close
your file. If you'd like me to keep you updated on new products
and promotions, I'm happy to do that too.

All the best,
[Name]
```

**为什么有效**: "Should I close your file?" 是高回复率的破冰句。

## 四、询盘回复关键策略

### 先辨别真伪

**高价值询盘特征**:
- 提到具体产品/规格/数量
- 使用专业术语
- 公司邮箱（非 Gmail/Yahoo）
- 能在 LinkedIn 找到此人

**低价值/钓鱼特征**:
- "I am interested in your products, please send catalogue and best price"
- 模糊不清，没有具体产品
- 免费邮箱，查不到公司信息
- 提出异常付款条件

### 回复黄金法则

1. **24小时内回复**（越快越好）
2. **逐一回答客户的每个问题**（不要遗漏）
3. **报价单要专业**: 产品图片 + 规格 + FOB价格 + MOQ + 交期 + 有效期
4. **提供额外价值**: 如推荐相关产品、分享行业趋势
5. **留下下一步**: "Would you like me to send samples?" / "When can we schedule a call?"

## 五、常用英文例句

### 开场/建立联系
- "We are writing to introduce ourselves as a leading manufacturer of..."
- "We learned about your company through [source] and were impressed by..."
- "I hope this email finds you well."

### 报价/产品介绍
- "Please find attached our latest catalogue and price list."
- "Our FOB price for [Product] is [Price] based on MOQ of [Quantity]."
- "We are pleased to quote as follows:"
- "This quotation is valid until [Date]."

### 样品相关
- "We would be happy to send you samples for evaluation."
- "Samples are free of charge; you only need to cover the shipping cost."
- "Please let us know your courier account number for sample delivery."

### 催单/催促回复
- "Just checking in to see if you've had a chance to review our proposal."
- "We're planning our production schedule and wanted to check if you have any updates."
- "Is there anything I can clarify about our quotation?"

### 表达不满（有礼貌地）
- "We regret to inform you that..."
- "We were disappointed to learn that..."
- "We would appreciate it if you could..."

### 涨价通知
- "Due to rising raw material costs, we need to adjust our pricing from [Date]."
- "We have absorbed cost increases for as long as possible, but now need to..."
- "To minimize the impact, we are giving you [X] days' notice before the new prices take effect."

### 结束语
- "We look forward to hearing from you."
- "Should you have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us."
- "Thank you for your time and consideration."
- "We look forward to building a long-term partnership with you."

## 六、不同国家开发信的语言偏好

| 国家 | 语言建议 |
|------|----------|
| 德国 | 德语标题效果更好（但英文也可） |
| 法国 | 法语问候 + 英文正文（法语加分巨大） |
| 日本 | 非常正式的英文，开头用 "Dear Sir/Madam" |
| 巴西 | 葡萄牙语（不要用西班牙语！） |
| 西班牙/南美 | 西班牙语问候 + 英文正文 |
| 俄罗斯 | 俄语标题 + 英文正文 |
| 中东 | 英文即可，注意避开宗教敏感词 |
| 其他 | 英文通用 |
