# Page Application

## Rule

Each page type needs a reference source and a page-specific verification pass. Do not apply one generic template to all pages.

## Home

- Primary reference type: premium visual/motion plus commercial adaptation.
- What to replicate: first-viewport composition, navigation confidence, section cadence, media scale, CTA rhythm, mobile first impression.
- What to adapt: target brand promise, real products/services, confirmed proof, commercial CTAs.
- Common AI failure: oversized generic hero, vague value props, repeated card sections.
- Required verification: desktop/mobile side-by-side, image loading, nav/menu states, no horizontal overflow.

## Products

- Primary reference type: commercial adaptation.
- What to replicate: grid density, product-card ratio, image consistency, filter/sort placement, hover behavior.
- What to adapt: real categories, product names, specs, prices only if confirmed, product imagery.
- Common AI failure: identical product cards with repeated images and fake specs.
- Required verification: product images load, card ratios match reference, mobile grid/stack works, no duplicate placeholder imagery.

## Product Detail

- Primary reference type: ecommerce/product reference plus premium layout reference.
- What to replicate: gallery/buybox balance, sticky purchase/inquiry behavior, spec hierarchy, FAQ/trust placement, mobile gallery-to-form stack.
- What to adapt: confirmed variants, specs, warranty, files, CTA, shipping/quote behavior.
- Common AI failure: decorative PDP with fake specs or broken add-to-cart/quote flow.
- Required verification: gallery images load, key CTA works, mobile buybox usable, no invented claims.

## Category / Collection

- Primary reference type: commercial adaptation.
- What to replicate: category intro, filter behavior, product-card rhythm, educational blocks, pagination/load behavior.
- What to adapt: real taxonomy, category-specific proof, buyer decision content.
- Common AI failure: generic grid with no buyer guidance.
- Required verification: filters/menu do not overflow, images complete, cards are varied and aligned.

## Service Page

- Primary reference type: Locomotive/Cuberto-style service hierarchy plus commercial adaptation.
- What to replicate: service promise, capability grouping, proof placement, process sequence, CTA rhythm, mobile service stack.
- What to adapt: real service scope, delivery process, buyer inputs, confirmed proof, limitations, and contact path.
- Common AI failure: vague "we help you grow" sections, abstract icons, fake platform/integration claims, and identical service cards.
- Required verification: desktop/mobile side-by-side, service claims confirmed or marked pending, form/CTA usable, no hidden proof gaps.

## Bag Style / Product Taxonomy Page

- Primary reference type: Rootree/CarePac-style commercial taxonomy plus premium visual reference.
- What to replicate: taxonomy tabs, product-style cards, category explanation rhythm, visual examples, CTA placement.
- What to adapt: target bag types, product images, use cases, material/printing notes only when confirmed.
- Common AI failure: icon-only bag categories, fake packaging renderings, repeated card templates.
- Required verification: each taxonomy item has relevant visual proof, mobile tabs/stack work, side-by-side comparison covers hero, taxonomy row, and card section.

## Capabilities / Factory / Operations Page

- Primary reference type: commercial B2B/factory plus editorial premium reference.
- What to replicate: process sequencing, large proof media, quality/control modules, capability grouping, restrained trust hierarchy.
- What to adapt: real factory/process/QC assets, confirmed machines/processes/capacity/certifications only.
- Common AI failure: fake factory claims, stock factory photos, generic "advanced equipment" copy.
- Required verification: no invented proof, images authorized/owned, mobile process sequence readable, forms/CTAs visible.

## Case Study / Project Page

- Primary reference type: premium project/editorial reference plus commercial proof reference.
- What to replicate: project hero, problem/process/result arc, media sequencing, detail captions, navigation to adjacent cases, ending CTA.
- What to adapt: real project facts, authorized images, confirmed outcomes, target-client confidentiality limits.
- Common AI failure: fake client logos, invented metrics, copied agency case-study rhythm without business substance.
- Required verification: no unconfirmed client/result claims, images authorized, desktop/mobile side-by-side, media captions readable, CTA route works.

## QR / Connected Packaging / Data Service Page

- Primary reference type: ePac Connected Packaging/commercial data service plus premium motion reference.
- What to replicate: service explanation sequence, diagram/data-flow placement, use-case rhythm, trust and CTA structure.
- What to adapt: target QR/data service scope, real capabilities, confirmed integration details.
- Common AI failure: fake dashboards, fake analytics, unsupported traceability claims.
- Required verification: claims marked confirmed or pending, diagrams do not imply unbuilt features, mobile diagrams readable.

## Contact / Quote / Lead Form

- Primary reference type: commercial lead-generation reference.
- What to replicate: form density, grouping, supporting proof, alternate contact paths, confirmation/error state.
- What to adapt: fields the business can actually use, privacy note, sales routing, file upload only if supported.
- Common AI failure: long decorative form, clipped mobile fields, vague "submit" CTA.
- Required verification: form is usable on desktop/mobile, fields fit, submit/validation states exist or are documented.

## About

- Primary reference type: premium editorial/service reference.
- What to replicate: company narrative cadence, founder/team/process imagery, trust placement, timeline rhythm if present.
- What to adapt: real company story, real people/factory/assets, confirmed timeline.
- Common AI failure: generic passion/innovation/excellence filler.
- Required verification: no fake history/team/client claims, mobile text measure readable.

## Quality / Trust

- Primary reference type: factory/commercial trust reference.
- What to replicate: proof hierarchy, certification/testing layout, process documentation, FAQ/support routing.
- What to adapt: confirmed quality process, certification files, test reports, warranty policies.
- Common AI failure: fake badges, fake certifications, unverifiable QA language.
- Required verification: every proof item is confirmed or marked pending, no fake metrics, images/doc links load.

## FAQ

- Primary reference type: commercial support/lead reference.
- What to replicate: grouping, accordion behavior, CTA after answer clusters, search/filter if reference uses it.
- What to adapt: target ordering, shipping, specs, service scope, production, quote process.
- Common AI failure: generic questions that do not answer buyer objections.
- Required verification: mobile accordion usable, answers do not invent policies/specs, CTA visible.

## Landing Page

- Primary reference type: premium visual/motion plus conversion reference.
- What to replicate: offer hierarchy, proof placement, objection handling, CTA cadence, form/checkout proximity.
- What to adapt: campaign-specific offer, real proof, legal claims, target conversion path.
- Common AI failure: fake urgency, fake discounts, generic hero and card stacks.
- Required verification: side-by-side first viewport, mobile CTA visibility, form/checkout path, no fake social proof.

## Long-Scroll Brand Page

- Primary reference type: premium motion/editorial reference.
- What to replicate: story arc, section transitions, media variety, scroll rhythm, ending CTA.
- What to adapt: target brand story, real product/process/proof visuals, commercial comprehension.
- Common AI failure: portfolio-like mystery that hides what the business sells.
- Required verification: scroll states captured, mobile simplified, CTAs remain findable, no repeated section template.
